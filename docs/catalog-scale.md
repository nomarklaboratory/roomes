# Physical catalog scale

Sources checked **11 October 2026**. One scene unit is one metre. Catalog sizes are **width × depth × height**, including the stand/base in the displayed posture. Original procedural models represent silhouette and scale; official product media provides material detail. The selected posture is illustrative, and the sample catalog does not establish rental availability.

## Preventing scale drift

`lib/catalog.ts` supplies dimensions to the builder, placement, and seated camera. Each product has a source, check date, variant and posture note. Models rest at Y=0 and are centered on their X/Z footprint. Accessories rest at the selected desk height. Chair Y geometry preserves the seat anchor without vertical stretching.

Compressed-asset checks compare all three axes to the catalog within **2 mm**, then verify origins, tabletop and seat anchors, seat dimensions, active-screen diagonal/aspect, versions, source evidence, and the 75 KB budget. Curved screens derive arc width from their chord and radius. Model version 4 invalidates the old catalog geometry. These checks validate geometry against catalog metadata; they do not turn an estimated foliage spread or a stylized surface into a measured replica.

Saved configuration version 2 retains the existing storage key. Version 1 product IDs and finishes migrate through `lib/catalog-migration.ts`, preserving instance IDs, duration and positions subject to the new products' placement bounds. Quotes continue to use the MVP's illustrative weekly rates.
